Welcome to D6 Pipeline

A companion app for Warhammer 40k that resolves attack pipelines so you can spend less time on math and more time on the table. Universal core mechanics — works for any army, any edition.

Quick start

  1. Pick a target at the top — a preset, a custom unit, or Raw mode for hit/wound counts only.
  2. Set up a weapon — Attacks, Damage, BS, Strength, AP. If multiple models in your unit fire the same weapon, bump the × N models stepper next to "Attacks."
  3. Tap Single Salvo. Each tap fires another salvo at the same target — damage accumulates until the unit drops or you press Reset.

Good to know

  • The Quick Roll tab is for moments when you just need to throw dice against a target.
  • Hit and wound modifiers cap at ±1 and re-rolls happen before modifiers, per the Core Rules.
  • Save a roster by name under Roster — it persists locally on this device.
  • Toggle Cover, Stealth, Feel No Pain, and other defensive abilities in Defensive Modifiers.
